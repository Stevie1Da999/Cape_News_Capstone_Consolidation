NEWS APPLICATION - DATABASE DESIGN

User
- Extends Django AbstractUser.
- Stores one role: Reader, Editor or Journalist.
- Reader subscriptions use many-to-many links to Publisher and Journalist users.

Publisher
- Stores name and description once.
- Many-to-many links connect Editors and Journalists to publishers.

Article
- Has one Journalist author.
- May have one Publisher or no Publisher for independent work.
- Stores title, content, creation date and approval state.

Newsletter
- Has one staff author.
- Many-to-many link to Articles so one article can appear in more than one newsletter.

This structure is normalized because user, publisher and article information is stored once and relationships are represented with foreign keys or many-to-many tables instead of duplicated text.
